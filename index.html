INCIDENT RESEARCH GUIDE

UNDERSTANDING AVIATION CRASHES

Accident investigations reconstruct what happened by examining several categories of evidence.

Aircraft & Systems

Investigators may examine aircraft configuration, maintenance history, engines, flight controls, navigation equipment, warnings, and other technical information.

Flight Sequence

The timeline can include departure, altitude, speed, routing, communications, deviations, emergency actions, and the final known flight path.

Weather & Environment

Visibility, wind, precipitation, icing, turbulence, terrain, runway conditions, and other environmental conditions may be relevant.

Human & Operational Factors

Crew workload, procedures, training, communication, air traffic control, dispatch, and operating conditions may form part of the investigation.

Evidence Recovery

Flight recorders, cockpit voice recordings, wreckage, photographs, radar data, communications, maintenance records, and witness information can contribute evidence.

Final Findings

Investigators may identify causes and contributing factors and issue findings or safety recommendations based on the evidence available.
